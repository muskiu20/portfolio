05 — questions

before you ask.

What do I actually get?

A live, deployed site — not a folder of files. You get the source code, hosting handed off or set up together, and a short handover call.

Do you work outside English?

Yes — most recently an English/Portuguese bilingual build with both scripts held on one baseline.

Do I choose the design?

You choose from a small set of real, working homepages rather than static mockups. Everything underneath the one you pick is my call.

What do you need from me to start?

A sense of who lands on the site and what you need them to do, any existing brand assets, and a deadline. I can propose the rest.

06 — process

how a project runs.

01

Brief

You tell me what the site has to do and who it's for. If the brief is thin, I'll make a call and tell you what I decided.

02

Direction

I build two or three real homepages, not mockups, drawn from your world rather than a style menu.

03

Build

I take the one you pick and build out every page by hand, tested at desktop and phone width.

04

Ship

It goes live with a real favicon, link preview and analytics, then I run one more pass to catch what the first screen missed.
