what's actually in it.

Type

Instrument Sans for structure, Instrument Serif for the handful of editorial moments — no more than two weights per family.

Structure

Six pages, hand-coded HTML and CSS with a small vanilla JS layer — no framework, no build step beyond image compression.

Signature

An offline-aware contact form that queues submissions in localStorage and syncs once the connection returns.

Motion

IntersectionObserver-driven reveals and a canvas-based hero animation, both gated behind prefers-reduced-motion.

Accessibility

Contrast checked to AA across both themes; every interactive element reachable and legible by keyboard alone.

Language

English and Portuguese held on one baseline, switchable per page without a layout shift.
